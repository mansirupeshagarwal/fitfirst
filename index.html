Questions

How do your sizes work?

One letter per inch, no doubles. Our E, F, G, H, I are UK DD, E, F, FF, G, in bands 28 to 38. Every label shows both.

I don't know my real size. Can you help?

Use the fit finder, measure with our short video, and if you're unsure a fitter can check your result on WhatsApp.

Can a G cup really wear a plunge or a halter?

Yes, when the band carries the weight. We'll tell you honestly which sizes each style suits.
